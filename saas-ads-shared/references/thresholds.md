# Threshold register

This file owns every numeric decision threshold in the pack. These thresholds are decision aids, not performance promises. A skill cites its T-ID instead of copying its number. Every threshold carries a unit and a tag: `[heuristic]` is this pack's working rule, `[platform guidance]` is a number the platform documents.

## Money and cohorts

- **T01, Volume Floor and Insufficient-Volume Verdict:** A finding about cost per paying customer, brand split, payback, a marginal cost, or a scale gate rests on at least 20 new paying customers per channel in the window (unit: customers per channel per window) [heuristic]. Below 20 the verdict is `needs_data`, never `monitor`, and the helper label is `insufficient_volume`. The number may be shown as arithmetic only: do not rank on it, act on it, or estimate it upward. Every other volume floor in this register (T02, T07, T08, T17, T19, T27, T28) returns the same verdict when it is missed. A small account that cannot reach 20 in a normal window uses T24.
- **T02, Trial To Paid By Campaign:** Count a signup in a paid-rate cohort only when its age is at least the trial length plus 7 days of billing lag (unit: days) [heuristic]. Compare campaigns only when each has at least 30 matured signups (unit: signups per campaign) [heuristic]. Younger signups are excluded and reported as immature.
- **T03, Weak Campaign Paid Rate:** Flag a campaign when its paid rate is at most 50% of the account paid rate (unit: ratio) and it meets the T02 volume floor [heuristic].
- **T04, Platform Versus Billing:** Escalate a source when the ratio of platform-reported conversions to billing-confirmed paying customers is above 1.25 or below 0.75 (unit: ratio) after dates, timezone, conversion definition, and attribution window match [heuristic]. It is a triage signal, not a measure of truth.
- **T05, Payback Period:** Review a channel when gross-margin payback is longer than 12 months, and hold scaling when it is longer than 18 months or never reached once churn is applied (unit: months) [heuristic]. Payback uses gross margin, never revenue. Without churn the helper returns `needs_churn`, because churn can only lengthen simple payback.
- **T21, Affordable Cost Per Paying Customer:** Affordable cost per paying customer is monthly gross margin per customer multiplied by n = 12 months, the T05 review line and the central figure (unit: currency per customer) [heuristic]. Never exceed monthly gross margin multiplied by n = 18 months, the T05 hold line and the ceiling. When churn is supplied, replace the product with m * (1 - (1 - c)^n) / c for n months, where m is monthly gross margin and c is monthly churn. `cac_recovery.py` prints both figures, and with `--churn-by-source` it prints the 12-month figure per source.
- **T24, Small Account Mode:** When a channel fails T01 in the normal window, widen to a rolling 90-day window that ends on the last matured date, and pool campaigns of the same platform and intent into one line (unit: days; customers per pooled line) [heuristic]. `--window-days 90` and `--pool name=a,b` in `cac.py` and `trial_to_paid.py` apply it. Never pool brand with non-brand, never pool across platforms, and label the line `pooled`. If the pooled line still has fewer than 20 paying customers, return `needs_data`. A pooled line cannot be split back out. For paid rate, pool campaigns below the T02 floor the same way, and apply T02 to the pooled line.
- **T25, Lagged CAC Cohort:** For a sales-led or demo-led account, compute lagged CAC as spend in click month M divided by paying customers whose click date falls in month M (unit: currency per customer) [heuristic]. Judge a click-month cohort only when the end of that month plus the 80th percentile of click-to-paid days is on or before the export date (unit: days); with no click-to-paid dates, use 90 days. A younger cohort is `immature` and returns `needs_data`. The customer list holds paying customers only, so recent clicks look faster than they are (right-censoring): `lag.py` takes the percentile and the T15 share only from clicks at least 90 days old (unit: days) [heuristic], and its `censoring:` line reports how many customers that leaves. `lag.py` also builds the cohort table.
- **T26, Blend Carrier and Unattributed Share:** A channel carries the blend when its share of paying customers exceeds its share of spend by at least 15 percentage points (unit: percentage points) [heuristic]. Unattributed customers can change a channel ranking when they are at least 10% of all paying customers (unit: share of customers) [heuristic]. A blank channel or campaign is read as `unattributed`. A row with zero spend and paying customers is also unattributed and prints n/a, never a cost. `cac.py` and `trial_to_paid.py` print a T26 line when the unattributed share reaches 10%.
- **T27, Marginal CAC:** Read the cost of the next dollar from two equal periods of at least 14 days around one budget change (unit: days) [heuristic]. Count customers by signup date, and count a period only when every signup in it is matured under T02: the export date must be on or after the last day of the after period plus the lag, where the lag is the trial length plus the billing lag (unit: days) [heuristic]. Compute incremental CAC as extra spend divided by extra paying customers, and read it only when spend rose at least 10% (unit: relative change) and the after period has at least 20 paying customers (T01) [heuristic]. The 10% minimum sits below the T18 step cap of 20%, so one T18-compliant step of 10% to 20% can be read; a larger past change is read as it happened but is not a step to repeat. Otherwise return `needs_data`. If extra spend added no customers the verdict is `over_affordable`. Compare incremental CAC with the T21 cost. It compares two periods, so it ignores season, offer, and tracking changes, and it is not incrementality. With no clean budget change, `marginal_cac.py --design` plans one step of 20% (the T18 maximum), a period of at least 14 days, and 20 paying customers in the after period.
- **T28, Sales-Cycle Maturity:** For a demo-led account, count a lead in show rate and close rate only when its age is at least the sales cycle (unit: days) [heuristic]. The sales cycle is the stated value, or the 80th percentile of lead-to-won days when at least 10 leads are won. Compare campaigns only when each has at least 30 matured leads (unit: leads per campaign) [heuristic]. A cost per won deal needs at least 20 won deals (T01). Younger leads are reported as immature, not as losses.

## Delivery and creative

- **T06, Meta Learning:** Meta says an ad set usually exits the learning phase after about 50 optimization events in a 7-day period since its last significant edit (unit: events per 7 days) [platform guidance]. Source: [About the learning phase](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700), checked November 2026.
- **T07, Meta Creative Fatigue:** Review an ad only after both 7-day windows have at least 3,000 impressions (unit: impressions per window). Flag a fatigue candidate when frequency rises at least 25% and CTR falls at least 20% between the two windows (unit: relative change) [heuristic]. Daily-row frequency near 1.0 to 1.2 is normal, so confirm a candidate with window-level frequency in Ads Manager.
- **T08, Search Term Waste:** Review a search term when it has at least 30 clicks, zero signups, spend of at least 2 times the target cost per signup (unit: currency), and a cleared conversion lag [heuristic].
- **T09, Brand Share:** Report brand and non-brand separately, and flag the blended number as misleading, when brand campaigns hold at least 40% of paying customers (unit: share of customers) [heuristic].
- **T17, Bid Target Volume:** Bid on the paid event only when the campaign has at least 30 paid conversions in the last 30 days (unit: conversions per 30 days) [heuristic]. Below that, bid on the deepest event that has at least 30 conversions in 30 days and compare it with the paid outcome. If no event reaches 30, return `needs_data`.
- **T18, Scale Step:** Change a budget by at most 20% per step and leave at least 7 days between steps (unit: percent; days) [heuristic]. Google and Meta publish no single required step size, so treat this as a conservative working rule.
- **T22, Not Learning:** Treat a campaign or ad set as out of learning only when all three hold: the status screenshot shows no learning label; no significant edit in the last 7 days (budget change above 20%, target or bid strategy change, conversion action change, new ad in the ad set); and the event volume meets its line, at least 50 optimization events in 7 days for Meta (T06) or at least 30 conversions in 30 days for Google (T17) (unit: days; events) [heuristic]. A missing screenshot returns `needs_data`.
- **T23, Results Rest On Brand Demand:** Say results rest on brand demand when brand campaigns hold at least 50% of the channel's paying customers, or when the non-brand cost per paying customer is at least 1.5 times the blended cost (unit: share of customers; ratio) [heuristic]. The non-brand line must also meet T01, or the verdict is `needs_data`.

## Tracking

- **T10, Offline Import Window:** Google Ads accepts an offline click-ID import up to 90 days after the click, and a user-data match (email or phone) up to 63 days (unit: days) [platform guidance]. Source: [Guidelines for importing offline conversions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15081888), checked November 2026. Verify against the current help page, since Google steers new setups to enhanced conversions for leads.
- **T11, Click ID Capture:** Flag the signup form or CRM when fewer than 80% of signups from paid clicks carry a stored click ID (`gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`, or `fbclid`) (unit: share of signups) [heuristic].
- **T12, Tracking Drop:** Escalate a tracking investigation when a primary event falls at least 30% while clicks stay within 10% of baseline over comparable 7-day windows (unit: relative change) [heuristic].
- **T13, Duplicate Meta Events:** Suspect double counting in the Meta layer when the browser plus server count for one event is at least 130% of the backend count for the same dates, after attribution differences are ruled out (unit: ratio) [heuristic]. Deduplication needs a matching event name and event ID on both sides. The GTM container layer and the GA4 or product layer have their own checks (see T20 for the GA4 layer).
- **T14, Consent Signal Loss:** Review consent handling when GA4 sessions for paid traffic are below 70% of ad clicks for the same dates and time zone (unit: share of clicks) [heuristic].
- **T15, Attribution Window Fit:** Flag a window as too short when more than 20% of paying customers convert later than the window after the click (unit: share of customers) [heuristic]. The verdict needs at least 20 paying customers (T01). `lag.py --window-days` prints the share and the median click-to-paid days.
- **T20, Signup Event Versus Backend Count:** Trace the firing paths of a GA4 or product `sign_up` event when events divided by backend signups for the same dates and time zone is above 1.20 or below 0.80 (unit: ratio) [heuristic]. The ratio is a trigger only. A capture of one real signup, from DebugView or Tag Assistant, confirms a double fire.

## Page and path

- **T16, Signup Form Friction:** Flag a self-serve signup when it asks for more than 5 required fields before the first product screen (unit: fields) [heuristic].
- **T19, Funnel Materiality Floor:** Rank a funnel stage as a bottleneck candidate only when, in both windows, at least 100 events enter the stage and at least 30 reach it (unit: events per stage per window), and its relative drop in stage rate is at least 10% (unit: relative change) [heuristic]. A stage below either floor returns `needs_data`; a drop below 10% is not material.

## Notes

- Cost per paying customer, paid rate, and payback are only as good as the billing export. A payment processor export beats a CRM stage when they disagree.
- Skills that decide whether to scale cite T01, T03, T04, T05, T12, T18, T21, T22, T23, and T27 together. No single threshold approves a budget change.
- Small accounts: T24 is the supported route when T01 fails. `weekly-readout-saas-ads` defaults to it.
- Trial-first maths: T02 and T03 assume a self-serve trial. Demo-led or sales-led accounts use T25 for lagged CAC, T28 for lead-to-won rates, and T05 with the annual-contract branch in `payback-period-check-saas-ads`.
